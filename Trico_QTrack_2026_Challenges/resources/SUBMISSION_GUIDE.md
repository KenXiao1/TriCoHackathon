# Preparing your completed QTrack notebooks

## Completed work

For every level your team attempted, save the `.ipynb` notebook with:

- Implemented functions in the marked `TODO` sections
- Visible self-check results, circuit diagrams, simulation outputs, and plots requested by the tasks
- Your answers to the written explanation questions
- A short description of your approach and any limitations you encountered
- An acknowledgment of substantial AI assistance (or a note that none was used)

Only include levels you worked on. Incomplete attempts can still show your reasoning and progress.

## Suggested filenames

- `TeamName_Level_0.ipynb`
- `TeamName_Level_1.ipynb`
- `TeamName_Level_2.ipynb`
- `TeamName_Level_3.ipynb`
- `TeamName_README.md` (optional; template included)

## Final checklist

1. Restart the notebook runtime and run the cells from the top in order.
2. Check that implemented tasks run without errors and self-checks report the expected results.
3. Retain the outputs, tables, and figures needed to understand your work.
4. Make sure your team can explain its code, including any AI-assisted work.
5. Remove passwords, access tokens, and personal data before sharing notebooks.
6. Save a copy of each completed `.ipynb` file. Keep the files together in a clearly named folder or ZIP if convenient.
