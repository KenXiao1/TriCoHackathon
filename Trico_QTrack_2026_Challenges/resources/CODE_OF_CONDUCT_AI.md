# Collaboration and AI use

The hackathon welcomes AI as a learning and development tool. Follow the official event AI Policy and Guidelines, which take priority over this informational page.

- You should understand and be able to explain your circuits, outputs and reports.
- Disclose material AI assistance (which tool/model, where and why used, and what you verified).
- Never put secret tokens, passwords or non-public information in AI prompts or public notebooks.
- Respect copyright and other teams' work.
- Explain discrepancies and limitations rather than hiding broken results.

Aim for reproducible results and clear explanations of what your team built and learned.
